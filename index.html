About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Engineering at Ewha Womans University, advised by Prof. Meejeong Lee. My research develops learning-based optimization frameworks—particularly deep reinforcement learning and topology-aware neural decision models—for communication-computation co-optimization in vehicular, edge, and next-generation wireless systems.

Core problems I address include task offloading under mobility and dynamic resource states, load-aware edge-cloud orchestration, and AI-driven wireless access control for heterogeneous networks. Expanding research directions include multi-RAT and heterogeneous network optimization, token communication, and semantic communication for next-generation wireless systems.

I am currently a Principal Investigator on a National Research Foundation of Korea (NRF) doctoral grant focused on RL-based resource optimization for vehicular computation offloading systems. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in February 2027 and am actively seeking postdoctoral opportunities.

Research Interests

Vehicular Edge Intelligence

Robust offloading and cooperation under dynamic topology and regional congestion in vehicular edge-cloud systems.

DRL-Based Resource Optimization

Learning-based frameworks for communication-computation co-optimization: scheduling, offloading, and load balancing.

Multi-RAT & Heterogeneous Networks

AI-driven wireless access control and resource management across heterogeneous radio access technologies.

Semantic & Token Communication

Goal-oriented and task-relevant communication paradigms for efficient information transmission in next-generation systems.
